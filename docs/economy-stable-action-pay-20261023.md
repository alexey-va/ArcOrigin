Picking up, using, fitting, adjusting, removing and returning borrowed gear count. The three successful brush strokes have separate ordinal receipts. Automatic handoff in GROOM/SADDLE, waiting, automatic route arrivals/checkpoints and final bookkeeping do not count. LESSON has one explicit rider handoff. There is no extra final-order bonus. Invalid or repeated clicks do not earn money.

Credits enter the existing serialized durable cashier with deterministic job/stage/ordinal IDs. They remain earned if a later part is cancelled, and settle at the end of the shift. The completion quest still advances once per real completed order. The existing shared 100-receipt allowance per UUID per UTC day remains at most 2,000 Vault across all side-job families. For the stable its visible counting unit becomes actions; the other families retain whole-order credits. The stored 20-Vault credit format and historical receipt amounts remain unchanged.

The time values and participation counts are explicit sensitivities. One observed owner SADDLE ran 12:21:15–12:24:42 (207 seconds) and earned 20; the following RATIONS took 13 seconds, then another long GROOM was offered. These log samples establish the reported ordering and poor reward per long task, not a repeatable ordinary hourly rate. The new dispatch gives each short variant weight 6 and each long variant weight 1, versus the previous uniform six-entry bag with three long variants. The initial eligible pool therefore has 1/7 long entries rather than 1/2. A minimum three-short-order gap applies between long-family orders, with an availability fallback only when no short variant can run.

The long-order deadline remains 480 seconds, other orders 240 seconds; the shift accepts work for 600 seconds and has 120 seconds grace. No per-click deadline extension is introduced. Partial-work payment increases emission from cancelled tasks, and higher pay reaches the existing common allowance sooner. This is accepted to pay for actual labor; it is not described as economically neutral. Even a fast valid-action loop cannot exceed 2,000 Vault per UUID-day. Multiple UUIDs still scale that existing ceiling linearly; no new account restriction or network budget is introduced.

# Forge cart steering and ground delivery

The 0.1.51 client report exposed two separate faults. The cart used the player's body heading, and reaching the forge chest only advanced to `HAUL_UNLOAD`; credit still required aiming at and clicking the elevated chest. Native read-only observation of GrocerMC at 2026-10-21 11:51:05 showed location/eye yaw `150.30119324°` and body yaw `200.30119324°`: a real 50-degree discrepancy, hidden by the former test fixture's equal yaw values.

The cart now uses `player.location.yaw` for its forward offset, cart and cargo rotation, and saved release yaw. The cart remains one metre ahead, with model -Z handles returning towards the worker. The item rotation includes Y+180 to cancel the native ItemDisplay base; release yaw is view yaw +180. Model, GROUND context, scale 3 and lift 0.5625 are unchanged.

This is grounded in the actual dependency, `arc-core-paper:2.7.41`, rather than the newer arc-core checkout: `PacketDisplay.frame` takes the mounted position from the carrier but yaw/pitch from the stored display position. Attached frames do not acquire the player's body yaw. The cached native 1.21.11 `Entity.rideTick`/`positionRider` code moves passenger position; it does not add the living body's heading to the display. The consumer therefore owns the display's authored heading.

## 0.1.53 correction

The live 13:35:12 haul followed two credited orders but never credited itself before cancellation at 13:36:26. This proves the missing completion; it does not identify the player's earlier exact coordinates. The corrected detector follows the owner's explicit player-X/Z rule. The dispatcher also removes the forced first `FORGE_NEW` order and retains existing random family/variant bags, stable offers and no immediate repeat at bag boundaries.

The Mila config reload at 12:43:52 exposed a separate lifecycle defect: `ModuleRegistry.shutdownAll()` clears its registrations, but ArcOrigin retained `modulesRegistered=true` and skipped registration on preparation. Runtime health consequently showed all ten modules down until the 12:58 restart. Preparation now always registers the ten modules after shutdown; the redundant flag is removed.

Reward policy remains 20 Vault per accepted whole order and 100 orders per UUID/UTC-day (2,000 Vault maximum). Geometry repair and random order do not award intermediate actions or change the settlement journal. The existing [cadence sensitivity assessment](economy-native-lead-cart-20261018.json) bounds a hypothetical 50% faster cadence; no new observed player income or timing is claimed.

The new regressions first failed on the old code: player inside the pad at +100Y was cancelled, direct clicking did not deliver, and cart-inside/player-outside incorrectly delivered. After correction, JDK25 focused verification passed 63 tests across `OriginOddJobForgeTest`, `OriginOddJobsGameTest`, `OriginOddJobsProgressTest`, `OriginOddJobsAssignmentTest` and `ArcOriginPluginGenerationTest`. The tests include spent admission resources, both entry paths, single credit and release, rotated pad boundaries, randomized starting families/variants and stable no-repeat bags. Lifecycle registration is source-reviewed against `arc-core:2.7.41`; the generic generation tests do not themselves run a full production reload. Native player acceptance still requires an observed post-activation haul.

Independent review found and closed a further pre-arrival cancellation: active `HAUL_PUSH` no longer revalidates spent admission resources, uses horizontal work-area distance, and does not recreate a missing cart. The expanded existing regression invalidates stock/NPC/cart/parking while the worker is still outside at +100Y, confirms the order remains active without credit, then enters at -100Y and credits once. The changed Forge/Game suites passed 41 tests again; the reviewer confirmed the previously failing case.
